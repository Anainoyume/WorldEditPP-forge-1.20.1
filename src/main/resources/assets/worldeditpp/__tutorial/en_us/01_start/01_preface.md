# [theme:Preface]
First of all, thank you very much for using this mod. This page explains what WorldEdit++ is meant to offer and who it is for.
If you only want to learn how to use it, feel free to skip this page and go straight to the [[theme:Quick Start]](01_start/02_quick_start.md "Open Quick Start").
> **[theme:Important]**: Almost all of this mod's code was written with AI. To be responsible to map creators, the mod will remain in testing until it has passed a large amount of in-depth testing. The current build is the internal 1.0.0 test version.

#### [theme:What does this mod provide?]
Its main features include visible selections, visual selection adjustment, fast editing of structures inside a selection, and smoother interactions.

#### [theme:Is this mod for me?]
WorldEdit++ is mainly a visual enhancement for WorldEdit. If you create huge, highly specialized builds, you may also want to look at Axiom.
If you already know WorldEdit, do not want to learn a completely new tool, or mainly need help with ordinary map creation, I believe WorldEdit++ can make the process more pleasant.
There is also another perfectly good answer: use Axiom and WorldEdit (with WorldEdit++) together. Why not? XD

#### [theme:Why was this mod created?]
Years ago, I made an RPG map for Minecraft 1.7.10. Building was never my strength, so I relied on WorldEdit to simplify as much work as possible. I played that map with friends over LAN and on servers, until school forced me to stop updating it. For a long time afterward, I did not create Minecraft maps at all.

One day, it felt like a shame to leave the map forgotten in a corner of my computer, so I uploaded it as a keepsake. To my surprise, some players downloaded and played it. Someone even made a video about it, thoroughly roasting the map I had built when I was young. I could only laugh along. I took the criticism seriously, and it made me want to create maps again. But Minecraft had changed quickly while my knowledge remained old, and my building skills were still just as poor.

I planned to return to WorldEdit, but kept wondering whether some of its actions could be less cumbersome. Around that time I learned about Axiom, an astonishingly powerful newcomer. I am not very good at leaving my comfort zone; that is one of my weaknesses. My friends and I already knew the WorldEdit workflow, and with little free time, learning such a large new mod felt daunting. Before learning a completely new creation tool, I asked AI to iterate on a companion mod that could make WorldEdit more efficient. That is how WorldEdit++ came to be.
