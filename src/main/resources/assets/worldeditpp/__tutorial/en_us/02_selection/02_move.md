@title Move
@subtitle Move your selection more quickly.

![Move mode](worldeditpp:textures/gui/selection_move_mode.png "width:32px height:auto title:Move")

#### [theme:Try moving it]
Switch your main hand to an empty slot, then press Alt + [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.sub_mode_move]] to enter Move mode. Three colored arrows appear at the center, with small square planes between them.
Aim at an arrow, **[theme:hold right-click, and move your view]**. The selection follows that arrow until you release the button. To move upward, for example, drag the green arrow:
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/12.jpg "title:The green arrow represents the Y axis height:70% width:70%")
:::image height:auto
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/13.jpg "title:Aim at the arrow")
⇨
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/14.jpg "title:Hold and drag upward")
:::

#### [theme:Arrows or square planes?]
An arrow moves along one direction only. Red corresponds to east/west, green to up/down, and blue to north/south. You do not need to memorize them; just watch the arrow you drag.
To move both sideways and forward at once, drag the small plane between two arrows. It moves along both axes and saves two separate drags:
:::image height:auto
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/15.jpg "title:Aim at the small plane")
⇨
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/16.jpg "title:Drag freely across the horizontal plane")
:::

When you walk around the selection, the arrows and planes turn toward the most convenient side. They hold still while you drag, so they will not suddenly flip and interrupt the action.

> **[theme:Remember]**: This moves only the selection. Its blocks remain where they are. To copy a house and move the copy, continue to [[theme:Adjustment Mode]](04_adjustment/01_begin.md "Open the Adjustment Mode chapter").

Happy with the location but not the size? Try [[theme:Stretch]](02_selection/03_stretch.md "Continue to Stretch").
