@title Stretch
@subtitle Quickly expand or shrink a selection face.

![Stretch mode](worldeditpp:textures/gui/selection_stretch_mode.png "width:32px height:auto title:Stretch")

#### [theme:Select a face]
Keep your main hand empty and press Alt + [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.sub_mode_stretch]].
Aim at a yellow selection face and it brightens slightly. **[theme:Right-click once]** to select it; the selected color is stronger. Right-click it again to cancel, or click another face to switch.
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/17.jpg "title:Hover over the face to select height:70% width:70%")
:::image height:auto
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/18.jpg "title:Right-click to select")
⇨
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/17.jpg "title:Right-click again to cancel")
:::

#### [theme:Stretch the selected face]
After selecting a face, hold [theme:Alt or Ctrl] and scroll the mouse wheel. Try one notch first to see the direction, then continue to the desired position.
An ordinary selection changes one block at a time. If the roof is missing one layer from the bounds, select the top and push it up one block. Scroll up to [theme:push away], down to [theme:pull closer].
:::image height:auto
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/18.jpg "title:With a face selected")
⇨
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/19.jpg "title:Pull the face closer (scroll down)")
:::

#### [theme:Need the far face?]
When the face you need is behind the building, hold [theme:Alt or Ctrl] while aiming at the selection. The highlight switches to the back face; right-click to select it.
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/20.jpg "title:Select the far face directly height:70% width:70%")

#### [theme:Resize every side]
Hold [theme:Ctrl + Alt] and scroll to expand or shrink all sides together. No face needs to be selected first.
Without these modifier keys, the wheel still switches hotbar slots. If stretching does nothing, check that your main hand is empty and that a face is selected.
:::image height:auto
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/21.jpg "title:Shrink all faces")
/
![](worldeditpp:textures/tutorial/22.jpg "title:Expand all faces")
:::

> A larger selection does not make the house larger. This still changes only the bounds, not blocks in the world.
