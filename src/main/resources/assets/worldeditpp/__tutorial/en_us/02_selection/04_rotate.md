@title Rotate
@subtitle Rotating bounds alone may have limited uses, but the option is here.

![Rotate mode](worldeditpp:textures/gui/selection_rotate_mode.png "width:32px height:auto title:Rotate")

#### [theme:Follow the rotating arrow]
With an empty main hand, press Alt + [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.sub_mode_rotate]].
Move the crosshair onto a selection face. A pale yellow rotating arrow appears at its center. Right-click once to rotate the selection [theme:90 degrees], or one quarter turn, in the shown direction.
To turn the other way, **[theme:hold Alt and right-click]**. The arrow itself reverses while Alt is held, so you can simply follow the preview.

> **[theme:This matters]**: Rotating an ordinary selection does not rotate the stairs, logs, or house inside it. To rotate the blocks too, use [[theme:Adjustment Mode]](04_adjustment/01_begin.md "Open Adjustment Mode").

Your selection can now be placed and sized precisely. Next, try [[theme:drawing blocks]](03_drawing/01_set.md "Open Drawing Mode").
