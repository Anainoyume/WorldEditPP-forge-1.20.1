@title Visual Selections
@subtitle WorldEdit++ can also serve as a selection visualizer.

#### [theme:Selections and the world]
After making a selection, you can adjust it if the bounds are not quite right. Use Alt + [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.sub_mode_move]] / [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.sub_mode_stretch]] / [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.sub_mode_rotate]] to enter [theme:Move] / [theme:Stretch] / [theme:Rotate] mode.
The other pages in this chapter explain each mode. Read them in any order, or simply experiment; WorldEdit++ aims to keep its interactions easy to understand.

Moving, stretching, or rotating an ordinary selection changes its bounds only. It does not move any blocks in the Minecraft world. The new bounds are synchronized with WorldEdit immediately, so commands such as `//set` use the adjusted selection.

#### [theme:Subdued outlines]
When your main hand is [theme:not empty] and [theme:not holding the wand], the yellow outline and corner markers become thinner and fainter. They return while your hand is empty, the wand is held, or the [theme:draw key] is down.
This keeps the selection from obscuring your view while you build. To remove it completely, see below.

#### [theme:Clear the selection]
Quickly double-tap [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.delete_selection]] to clear the selection.
