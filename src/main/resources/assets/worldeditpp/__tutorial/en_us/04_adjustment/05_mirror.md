@title Mirror
@subtitle Often cuts your work in half, and sometimes by three quarters.

![Adjustment Mirror](worldeditpp:textures/gui/mirror_mode.png "width:32px height:auto title:Mirror (Adjustment)")

#### [theme:Choose a face as the mirror]
Suppose you have built the left half of a gate and want a symmetrical right half. Put the left half inside the yellow selection and press [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.mirror_adjustment]] to enter mirror selection immediately.
You can press the same key while already adjusting a white projection.
Keep your main hand empty and aim at a face. A moving grid, similar to the world border, marks it as the mirror. **[theme:Right-click that face]** and the copy flips to its other side.

#### [theme:Cancel or keep adjusting]
Press [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.mirror_adjustment]] again while choosing a mirror to return to ordinary adjustment.
After one successful mirror, the mode also returns automatically. You can move the result, mirror again, then press [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.confirm_adjustment_enter]] to place it, or [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.adjust_clipboard]] to cancel the whole projection.

> The end of the mirror animation does not place anything. As long as the projection is white, it can still be changed or cancelled.

Finally, learn to keep a structure in the [[theme:clipboard]](04_adjustment/06_clipboard.md "Open Copy, Paste, and Clipboard") and use it farther away.
