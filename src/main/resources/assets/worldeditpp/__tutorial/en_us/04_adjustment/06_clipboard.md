@title Copy, Paste, and the Clipboard
@subtitle This may be one of the most "useful" features.

![Adjustment after paste](worldeditpp:textures/gui/move_mode.png "width:32px height:auto title:Pasting enters the adjustment state")

#### [theme:Copy]
Enclose a structure in the yellow selection and press **[theme:Ctrl + [key:key.worldeditpp.adjust_clipboard]]**. WorldEdit++ uses WorldEdit's copy operation, equivalent to `//copy`, and stores those blocks in the WorldEdit [theme:clipboard].
Think of the clipboard as a temporary place for one structure. Copying does not remove the original and does not immediately enter projection adjustment. Copying another selection replaces the clipboard's previous contents.

#### [theme:Paste]
Walk to the target area and press **[theme:Ctrl + [key:key.worldeditpp.paste_clipboard]]**. The clipboard appears as a white projection roughly in front of you.
Now use Move, Stretch, Rotate, and Mirror as before. Press [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.confirm_adjustment_enter]] when ready, or hold [theme:Alt] while confirming to skip air.

## [theme:Summary]
You can now define an area, draw and replace materials, and copy, repeat, rotate, or mirror complete structures. Try the tools on your own work, and spend more of your time creating.
