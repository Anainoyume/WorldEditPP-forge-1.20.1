@title Move a Copy
@subtitle Copy a structure and place it nearby.

![Adjustment Move](worldeditpp:textures/gui/move_mode.png "width:32px height:auto title:Move (Adjustment)")

#### [theme:Drag it into place]
After entering the adjustment state, leave your main hand empty and press Alt + [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.sub_mode_move]].
It works like ordinary selection [[theme:Move]](02_selection/02_move.md "Review selection movement"): hold right-click on an arrow to move along one axis, or drag the plane between arrows to move along two.
This time, the blocks inside the white outline move with it. Select a window, press [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.adjust_clipboard]], and drag the copy aside to position a second window.

#### [theme:The copy moves, not the original]
The projection is a copy of your selected blocks. It does not cut the original window out of the wall, and the copy is not real until you press [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.confirm_adjustment_enter]].
Keep dragging until the position is right, or press [theme:[key:key.worldeditpp.adjust_clipboard]] again to cancel. Previewing cannot damage the original.
> To move rather than copy, place the projection and then fill the original yellow selection with air. The yellow selection keeps its original size and position while adjustment is active; it is only hidden.

To make many identical windows at once, continue to [[theme:Repeat by Stretching]](04_adjustment/03_stretch.md "Open projection stretching").
